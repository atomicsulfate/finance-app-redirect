FinanzApp privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026.

FinanzApp is a private app used by the members of one household. This page describes what the app does with data.

Data the app handles

  • Bank account data (balances, transactions) that the household imports from files or retrieves through the bank-access provider Enable Banking after the account holder's explicit consent.
  • The user's own edits: categories, notes, splits and rules.

Where the data is stored

  • On the user's own devices, in the app's private storage.
  • In a folder of the user's Google Drive, so that the household's devices can synchronise. Everything written to Drive is encrypted on the device with a key derived from a passphrase known only to the household. Google and the app developer cannot read it.

Use of Google user data

The app requests access to Google Drive only to create, read, update and delete the files in its own synchronisation folder. It does not read, copy or transfer any other file. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

The app does not sell or share data with third parties and does not include advertising or analytics. The only external services contacted are Google (Drive, for synchronisation), Enable Banking (to retrieve bank data the user has authorised) and, only when the bank requires the address of the person's device for a request made by that person, a public IP-address lookup service (api.ipify.org), which receives nothing but the connection itself. Bank data is never sent to anyone else.

Retention and deletion

Data stays until the user deletes it. Deleting the synchronisation folder from Google Drive and uninstalling the app removes all copies. Bank access can be revoked at any time from the bank or at enablebanking.com/data-sharing-consents, and Drive access from the Google account's security settings.
